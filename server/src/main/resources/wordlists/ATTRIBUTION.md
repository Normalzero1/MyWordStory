# Word list attribution

`candidates.csv` merges three lists by Charles Browne and colleagues (each list's page names its authors),
downloaded from https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com and used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/):

- NGSL-Spoken 1.2 (721 words)
- Business Service List (BSL) 1.20 (1,744 words)
- TOEIC Service List (TSL) 1.2 (1,250 words)

Changes: the three `*_stats.csv` files were merged, duplicate words removed (3,140 left; "TRUE" fixed to "true"),
`sources` lists which of the three lists each word comes from, and `rank` is the best rank
the word has in any of them. Under the ShareAlike terms, `candidates.csv` is also CC BY-SA 4.0.
